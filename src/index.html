Three programs

Two master's programs and a Ph.D., all taught in English. Each master's program takes 2 years and the Ph.D. takes 2 to 4 years depending on the study plan.

See the courses and requirements

Who the program is for

Doctors who want to specialize in dermatology, do research or develop new treatments. Graduates also learn to set up and run their own dermatology practice.

What you need to apply

A Doctor of Medicine degree with a GPA of at least 2.50 for the master's, and an English score such as IELTS 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 61. Thai applicants also need a medical license with two years of experience, including at least one year of internship.

Clinical training from the first semester

Students attend lectures and treat real patients in the clinic under experienced instructors. They also train at partner hospitals across Thailand.

  • Clinic from semester one

    Dermatology Clinic 1 starts in the first semester, and Hands-on Experiences in Dermatology runs in the second year.

  • A thesis in both years

    Every master's student writes a thesis, and graduates have published their work in international journals.

  • Research methods early

    Research ethics, statistics and journal club are taught in the first semester.
