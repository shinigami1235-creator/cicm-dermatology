Entry requirements

Master's

  1. A Doctor of Medicine degree from a university in Thailand or abroad that the University Council approves.
  2. A cumulative GPA of at least 2.50.
  3. Thai applicants need a medical license, two years of experience and at least one year of internship before the application date.
  4. General qualifications approved by the program committee.
  5. Foreign applicants meet items 1, 2 and 4.

Ph.D.

  1. Plans 1.1 and 2.1 (with a master's): a master's in dermatology or an equivalent science degree with a GPA of at least 3.00.
  2. Plans 1.2 and 2.2 (with an M.D.): a GPA of at least 3.50.
  3. Thai applicants need a medical license and at least one year of internship.
  4. An English score from TU-GET, TOEFL or IELTS, no older than two years on the application date.
  5. CICM students in M.Sc. Clinical Dermatology (Plan A2) with at least 24 registered credits and an M.D. GPA of at least 3.50 can apply for Plan 2.2.
  6. Foreign applicants follow the admission committee's criteria.

Selection

A written exam on basic dermatology and an interview.

Documents to send

  1. Application form
  2. Copy of ID card or passport
  3. Copies of degree certificates
  4. Official transcript
  5. Medical license
  6. Internship certificate
  7. Official English score report (TOEFL, TU-GET or IELTS)
  8. Updated CV
  9. Three recommendation letters, one from your current supervisor

Tuition

Per semester. Accident insurance is 110 THB a year.
